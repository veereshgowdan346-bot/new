## 3. Paper Execution & Regulatory Charge Accounting

### 3.1 Option Turnover & Spot Index Quarantine
In earlier versions, spot index levels (such as BANKNIFTY spot 51,800) were occasionally routed to options fee calculations, causing grotesque turnover overstatements ($51,800 \times 15 = ₹7,77,000$ instead of $₹320.50 \times 15 = ₹4,807.50$).

The hardened engine enforces:
1. `validate_option_contract_integrity(symbol, price, strict=True)`:
   - If `symbol` lacks strike/expiry format (e.g. `BANKNIFTY` instead of `BANKNIFTY26OCT51800CE`), the record is flagged `is_quarantined = True`, `validation_status = "QUARANTINED"`, and `charges_status = "UNRESOLVED"`.
   - All 102 historical option records in `server/chintu_memory.db` lacking strike metadata are safely held in Level 2 quarantine with total charges ₹0.00 and excluded from bot qualification.
2. For valid option contracts, turnover is strictly $Premium \times Qty$.

### 3.2 Historical Accounting Reconciliation
All 759 records in `server/chintu_memory.db` remain partitioned into 4 mutually exclusive levels:

$$759 = 267 \text{ (Level 1 Clean)} + 102 \text{ (Level 2 Quarantined)} + 4 \text{ (Level 3 Emergency)} + 386 \text{ (Level 4 Non-Trades)}$$

Where Level 4 Non-Trades comprise:
- 198 RMS Pre-Order Rejections
- 122 Initial Entry Orders
- 66 Mock Broker Test Doubles

Closed trades evaluate to:
$$373 = 210 \text{ Wins} + 159 \text{ Losses} + 4 \text{ Breakevens}$$

Mathematical identity $Gross - Charges = Net$ holds across all clean closed trades.
